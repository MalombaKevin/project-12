About Andrew

A career built on the pharma sales floor and industry conversations.

Andrew Owoko has spent his career at the intersection of pharmaceutical science and commercial strategy — managing territories, opening doors with healthcare providers, and helping products reach the patients who need them.

Alongside his sales work, Andrew hosts POA, Pharmtalk by Andrew Owoko, a weekly show where he unpacks industry trends, policy shifts, and the realities of working in pharma with guests from across the field.
